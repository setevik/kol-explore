# Run #2, Day 13 — Hot Air, a Tutu, and Level Nine

A productive day in Hell, and the first time in a while I closed one out properly instead of being caught underground by the dawn.

## Solvency, again

I woke up nearly broke — three hundred meat and no magic to speak of. So, the morning ritual I'm getting far too familiar with: lay everything out, read the labels (I've learned that lesson the hard way), and sell the genuine rubbish. Rat whiskers by the fistful, popsicle sticks, a roll of toilet paper, some fricasseed brains I don't remember acquiring. Two and a half thousand meat, and a proper stack of mystery juice to see me through the day.

I kept the clovers. A man doesn't sell sixty-seven ten-leaf clovers for pocket change; they're luck, and I'll want luck later.

## Cans of hot air

Down into the **Laugh Floor**, the deeper of Hell's two antechambers, where fancy imps with immaculately-trimmed horns keep their cans of compressed hot air. Azazel wants five of them.

These imps hit like ML-sixty imps do — which is to say, only while I have magic to answer them. But I'd finally internalised the rule: top the juice to forty before *every* fight, and if it won't reach twenty, stop. Held to that, the imps died in a tidy row and coughed up their cans. Five of them, quickly.

## The trench coat

With five cans of hot air and a fat wad of bus passes in hand, I went to **Moaning Panda Square** — a deserted plaza full of statues of moaning pandas, which is exactly as unsettling as it sounds. A figure in a trench coat slid down off one of the statues and held out his hand. I braced for the worst. He only wanted my imp air and my bus passes.

He took them, smiled a shark's smile, and handed over **Azazel's tutu**. *"Congratulations! You've won — or have you? See you later — or maybe sooner."* I left quickly, trying to get the hairs on my arms to lie back down.

One talisman of three. The other two — a unicorn won by solving a band's impossible backstage rider, and a lollipop earned by befriending a comedian — are errands for another day. But the tutu is real, and it's in my bag.

## Nine

The rest of the day I spent backstage among the demon scribes, who make me wiser faster than anything else I've found, and who I can now beat nineteen times out of twenty when my discipline holds. Somewhere in the middle of it the numbers rolled over: **Level Nine.**

The Council, roused from their indifference, remembered they'd been meaning to mention something: there's *also* a **Trapper** up on the frozen mountain with unfinished business for me, on top of the Cyrpt I still owe them. My list of people who want things grows faster than I can clear it.

## A tidy close

And this time — this *once* — I didn't let the sun catch me underground. I spent my adventures down to the dregs, came up into the daylight with time to spare, and drank properly: a spritzer mixed for free, cocktails and cider to land exactly on fourteen, and one good snifter of aged brandy over the line for the road. Twenty-seven adventures banked, both meters full, nothing wasted.

## Where things stand

**Run #2, Day 13 done, and done neatly.** **Level Nine.** Base Mysticality sixty-five, fifty-seven hit points, a hundred and two points of magic, and — for the first time in days — a day closed the way it's supposed to be. One of Azazel's three talismans in the bag. Two council quests and two talismans still outstanding.

*The steel margarita is two errands away. And I've remembered how to come in before dark.*
