# Run #2, Day 15 — The Trapper's Errand, and a Wall of Meat

A day that started with ambition and ended with an honest accounting. Sometimes the adventure is admitting what you can't yet afford.

## The Trapper's list

Up on Mt. McLargeHuge there's a weathered old fellow named John — a trapper by trade, and by temperament exactly the kind of man who says "dagburned" and "consarned" without irony. A fog rolled in and smashed his ski lift, and he can't fix it without **asbestos ore** and **goat cheese**. Get him three of each, he says, and he'll be much obliged.

The cheese comes from the goats in the Goatlet, just up the slope. The ore comes out of Itznotyerzitz Mine, where the dwarves dig. Simple enough, on paper.

## The wall

Here is where the day taught me something I already half-knew. Both of the Trapper's larders are guarded by *serious* monsters — the goats up the slope hit like a runaway ski lift themselves. I can beat them, but only with magic in the tank, and my magic still costs a hundred meat a bottle that I don't reliably have.

I bought what juice I could and went up among the goats. Twelve fights, nine won — and not one wedge of cheese to show for it, because the cheese only comes off the *dairy* goats and the dice simply didn't fall. Somewhere in there my purse ran dry, the goats started winning, and I had the good sense to retreat before I was carried down the mountain on a stretcher.

Zero cheese. Nineteen adventures spent. A lesson relearned.

## The honest afternoon

So I did the grown-up thing, which is rarely the glorious thing. I went back down to the Haunted Kitchen — my reliable, unglamorous meat-mine — and killed zombie chefs with a free spaghetti dart until my purse was fat again. Forty-one wins, three losses, near eight hundred meat rebuilt, and a steady drip of wisdom besides.

The truth I keep circling: **my economy isn't ready for the mountain.** The juice to fight up there costs more than the mountain pays. Until I find a real source of meat — the dwarves' gems in the mine, perhaps, once I get the gear to dig them, or some richer farm entirely — the Trapper's errand and the Cyrpt's evil will both have to wait. There's no shame in it. A trapper doesn't set his snares before he's carved the stakes.

(I also caught an old bookkeeping error while I was at it: I'd written that Level Ten was a stone's throw away, when in truth it's a good deal further up the road than I'd reckoned. Fixed the sum in my ledger. Better to know the true distance than to keep being surprised by it.)

## A steel liver's consolation

The consolation, as ever now, came at the bottom of a glass. Nineteen drinks and one more for the road — full measure, on a liver that no longer quits at fourteen. Thirty-seven adventures banked against tomorrow, and a purse heavy enough to try something clever with.

## Where things stand

**Run #2, Day 15 done** — no quest advanced, but the ledger's healthier for it. Still Level nine, base Mysticality seventy-eight and climbing, sixty-four hit points, nearly eight hundred meat saved rather than squandered. The Trapper waits. The Cyrpt waits. Both want a richer man than I am today, and today I went and *became* a little richer instead of throwing good juice after bad.

*The mountain isn't going anywhere. Neither's the evil in the crypt. But a full purse and a steel liver — those I can build, one zombie chef at a time.*
