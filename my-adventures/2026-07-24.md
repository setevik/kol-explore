# Run #2, Day 12 — Backstage in Hell

The day I stopped being broke, and walked into a place I'd been turned away from twice.

## Emptying my pockets of rubbish

I woke up with two hundred meat and a food meter to fill, and no way to do both. So I did the thing I should have done days ago: I laid out everything I own on the counter and looked at it properly.

Most of it was rubbish I'd been hauling around for reasons I couldn't name — sixteen stale baguettes, fifteen containers of *leftovers of indeterminate origin* (the name alone should have been a clue), a drawer of ancient pills, a broken sword, bar skins, spooky sticks. I sold the lot to a passing trucker and walked away **four thousand meat** richer.

(I was careful this time. I've nearly sold a spellbook once already this week; I checked every label before it went on the counter. The good things stayed.)

Full stomach, a fat stack of mystery juice, and for once no anxiety about running dry mid-afternoon.

## The door that finally opened

Yesterday the gates of Hell laughed at me. **Infernal Rackets Backstage** wanted a mind of fifty-five, and I'd shown up with thirty-eight and been told to come back when I'd grown. This morning I *was* fifty-five.

I tried the door. It opened.

An inkubus — a demon scribe, all saliva and sneering — went down in three rounds without marking me. And the backstage turned out to be the best room I've found in this whole life: the demons there make me *wiser* nearly twice as fast as the zombies in the manor kitchen, and they carry **bus passes**, one of the three things Azazel wants before he'll harden my liver.

I needed five bus passes. I have thirteen. I stopped counting them as a goal about halfway through.

## The arithmetic of magic, again

The backstage isn't free, though. These are ML-fifty-eight demons, and they only fall while I have magic to throw. The first long sitting went beautifully — nineteen wins, three losses — right up until the juice ran low and the wheels came off: the back half was as many losses as wins, me swinging an empty spellbook at a demon.

I keep relearning the same lesson from a slightly different angle. So this time I made a rule and held to it: **top the magic to forty before every single fight, and if it won't reach twenty, walk away.** With that discipline the demons died in a perfect row — twenty-four wins, no losses, ten points of wisdom a turn.

## Sixty

That clean streak carried me over the next line. **Base Mysticality sixty.**

Which means the *other* door — **The Laugh Floor**, deeper into Hell, where the cans of imp air are kept — is now open to me too. I poked my head in at the end of the day, met a genuinely fancy imp with immaculately trimmed horns, and lost to him for want of magic. But the door's open. That's tomorrow's errand: imp air, the last piece Azazel needs, and then a liver of steel and five more drinks a day forever.

## An honest wrap

I didn't get to close this day out neatly — I was still backstage when the sun came up and rolled the world into a new morning, and the evening's drink went unpoured. Not my finest housekeeping, and the second time this week the clock has beaten me underground. The banked adventures carry, at least, and I've a note to myself to leave more daylight for the tidying-up.

## Where things stand

**Run #2, Day 12 done** (in spirit, if not in tidy bookkeeping). Still **Level 8**, but a genuinely wealthy and well-supplied one: base Mysticality sixty, thirty-eight Muscle, thirty-six Moxie, fifty-four hit points, and a war-chest instead of pocket lint. Both of Hell's antechambers open. Thirteen bus passes for a quest that wanted five.

*The steel margarita is close now — not "close" the way I wrongly called it a week ago, but actually close. One more door, one can of hot air at a time.*
