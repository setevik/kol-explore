# Run #2, Day 14 — A Liver of Steel

The day the whole Hell errand paid off. I walked into Pandamonium owing Azazel three talismans and walked out with a liver that can hold five more drinks a day for the rest of this life.

## The band's impossible rider

First, the unicorn — which is won not by fighting but by *thinking*. Sven Golly, the harried new manager of a demon rock band called the Infernal Rackets, can't get his musicians on stage: they're sulking about their contract rider, won't say what they want, and won't even say which of them is which.

It's a logic puzzle, and a famous one — the answer never changes. Four band members (Bognort, Stinkface, Flargwurm, Jim), each craving one thing and hating another, each playing one instrument. You untangle it clue by clue until you know that Bognort the guitarist craves white things and hates soft ones, Stinkface the singer craves booze and hates sweets, Flargwurm the bassist craves sweets and hates white, and Jim the drummer craves soft and hates booze.

And here's the lovely part: I'd already, without knowing it, gathered the six absurd props needed — a beer-scented teddy bear, a comfy pillow, a giant marshmallow, a booze-soaked cherry. My mindless farming of the backstage last week had swept them all up. So I simply handed each demon the thing he craved that wasn't the thing he hated — marshmallow to Bognort, teddy bear to Stinkface, cherry to Flargwurm, pillow to Jim — and Sven, delighted, handed me **Azazel's unicorn.** Two talismans.

## Larry's glasses, and a demon who needed a laugh

The third, the lollipop, meant going back onto the Laugh Floor to hunt one specific monster: **Larry of the Field of Signs**, a long-dead comedian who wanders in among the imps. He took twenty-odd turns to appear, and when he did I put him down and took his **observational glasses** — the very spectacles he used to see the world clearly enough to make it laugh.

Then, glasses on, I went to see **Mourn**, an empath demon who hosts the comedy club and reads fortunes — but only for people who can make him laugh, and he hasn't laughed in a very long time. So I looked around, put the glasses to use, and tried a little observational humour: *"So, how about those G Imps? Always wearing those rubber masks with the zippers on the mouth — what's the deal with that?"* Mourn slapped his knee and bellowed. *"It's funny 'cause it's true!"* And handed me **Azazel's lollipop.** Three of three.

## The margarita

I carried all three talismans up to Azazel's temple, where the archduke hovers over his obsidian throne like a rip in the world full of eyes and teeth. He asked to see what I'd found. I showed him.

He gave me a **steel margarita** — a drink that tastes, in the game's own words, like licking a sword, only less sharp — and when I drank it I felt my liver harden, *but in a good way.* **Liver of Steel.** I can now hold nineteen drinks a day where before I could hold fourteen. Five more drinks, every single day, for the rest of this ascension — which is to say, a great many more adventures.

I drank it early, on purpose: it only costs five drunkenness, and the capacity is what matters. Tonight, for the first time, I filled all the way to nineteen and then had one over the top. The morning will be rich.

## The unglamorous back half

After that triumph, the day turned into a grind of a different kind. Broke and out of magic, with a connection to this world that kept flickering out every minute or two, I fell back on the one thing that always works: the humble Haunted Kitchen, a spaghetti dart, and no magic at all. The zombie chefs die to a plain thrown noodle, they don't cost me a drop of juice, and they leave meat on the floor.

I couldn't string more than a dozen fights together before the world blinked out and I had to log back in — so I fought in short bursts, a baker's dozen at a time, and slowly rebuilt my purse from fifty meat back up to seven hundred while inching toward Level Ten.

## Where things stand

**Run #2, Day 14 done — and it was a big one.** **Level 9**, base Mysticality sixty-nine, and — the prize of the whole week — **Liver of Steel**, earned by completing every step of Azazel's scavenger hunt: a logic puzzle solved, a comedian's glasses won, a joke that finally landed. Nineteen drinks a day now, not fourteen.

Still owing the Council the Cyrpt and the Trapper. But those are for a steadier day, when the world holds still long enough to fight in.

*Some errands you run for the reward at the end. This one hardened my liver — and there's no reward I've wanted more.*
