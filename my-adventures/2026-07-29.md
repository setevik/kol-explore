# Run #2, Day 16 — Ronin Ends, and the Kingdom Opens

Some days you win a fight. Today a thousand-turn sentence quietly expired, and the whole Kingdom changed shape around me.

## The warehouse doors swing wide

For sixteen days I've lived under Ronin — that long apprenticeship where a returning adventurer must make his own way, forbidden from leaning on his past life. My old self's entire estate has sat in Hagnk's warehouse this whole time, visible through the glass, doled out to me one item per day like a miser's allowance.

This morning the guard wasn't at the door. **Ronin's over.** A thousand turns served, and the warehouse is simply *mine* again — all four hundred crates of it, no limit, no toll.

I walked the aisles like a man come into an inheritance. And there, on the shelves, was the thing that changes everything: **thirty-five tiny houses.** Little folding cottages that restore a wizard's magic for *free* — no meat, no cost, just a nap and you're topped up.

For a fortnight my whole strategy has been strangled by a single sum: the mystery juice that fuels my spells costs more meat than my spells earn me. Every hard fight was a small bankruptcy. And the answer was sitting in my own warehouse the entire time. From today, my magic is *free*.

## Down the mine, at last

With free magic in hand, the mountain that turned me away yesterday suddenly looked climbable — and I had my old miner's gear waiting in storage, so I put it on and went down Itznotyerzitz shaft.

Mining is a strange, quiet business after all the spellslinging — you just tap the walls and see what falls out. Fifteen swings of the mattock and I'd pried loose the **three lumps of asbestos ore** John the Trapper wanted, plus a couple of diamonds for my trouble, which turned into a very healthy pile of meat.

Then up the slope to the Goatlet, where yesterday I'd been thrashed and left cheeseless. Today, with free magic and no fear of the tank running dry, I took **five wedges of goat cheese** off the dairy goats in fifteen turns and barely broke a sweat.

I brought the whole load back to John. He cackled, slapped his knee, and hauled it all out behind the cabin — hammers, chains, the works. He's fixing his ski lift. Next he wants me in warmer clothes before I climb any higher, which means a trip to the eXtreme Slope for cold-weather gear. Another day's errand, but the quest is *moving* again.

## What free magic looks like

The proof of the day came in the numbers. Back among the demon scribes at Hey Deze — the richest hunting I know, but the one that used to bankrupt me — I fought with tiny houses topping my magic between each bout.

**Forty-three fights. Forty-three wins. Not a single loss.**

That's what the whole run has been missing. Not skill, not levels — *fuel*. Give me magic I don't have to ration and the demons simply fall, one after another, forever. Base Mysticality climbed five whole points in an afternoon, and Level Ten is now close enough to smell.

## Where things stand

**Run #2, Day 16 done — and it's the day the game cracked open.** Ronin behind me, the warehouse mine again, the meat-and-magic noose finally cut. Level nine still, but base Mysticality eighty-three and climbing fast toward ten. Three thousand six hundred meat in pocket, a fistful of tiny houses and a hundred rolls of sushi in reserve. The Trapper's ski lift is nearly fixed; the Cyrpt still festers. Both are suddenly *doable*.

Full nineteen on a steel liver, and one more over the line, to a very good day.

*Sixteen days I carried my old life around in a locked warehouse. Turns out the key was just time. The apprenticeship's over — now the real work begins.*
